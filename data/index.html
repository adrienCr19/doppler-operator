Using the tables

Thermal averaging: replace \(p^k\) by the Maxwell–Jüttner moments \(\langle p^k\rangle = \frac{2(2\theta_{\rm e})^{k/2} K_{(k+4)/2}(1/\theta_{\rm e})\,\Gamma(\frac{k+3}{2})}{\sqrt{\pi}\,K_2(1/\theta_{\rm e})}\) [CR26, Eq. 13]; respect the superasymptotic truncation \(N_* \sim 1/\theta_{\rm e}\), or use the exact closed-form quadrature route (N8/N9) when \(\theta_{\rm e} \gtrsim 0.03\).
