Closed-form catalog of the Doppler operator — generated by the pure-D ladder

Every Doppler operator \(\Do^0_{\ell\ell'\ell''}(\nu,\beta)\) up to \(\Do_{555}\) below is produced from the single seed \(\Do_{000}\) by the recurrence ladder of the Doppler operator itself — the outer axis ladder (P0), the middle-index linear recurrence (P3′), reflection (N1) and the rank-one fill (P2). The aberration kernel is never used to generate anything; it enters only as an independent numeric check. Every element reduces to the border tower \(\Do_{\ell\ell'0}\) through

\[ \Do_{\ell\ell'\ell''}=\frac{\Do_{\ell\ell'0}(\Oh)\,\Do_{\ell''\ell'0}(3-\Oh)}{\Do_{0\ell'0}(\Oh)}\qquad\text{(P2 + N1)}, \]

and the border tower is climbed from \(\Do_{000}\) by P0 (raise \(\ell\)) and P3′ (raise \(\ell'\)).

Everything below is an operator identity in \(\Oh=-\nu\partial_\nu\) [CR26; Lemma L1; N12]

The Doppler operator acts on a spectrum, so the physical object is the operator-valued expression in the energy-shift generator \(\Oh=-\nu\partial_\nu\) — not the power-law label. Accordingly the entries are written with the operator bracket

\[ J_{w-\Oh}\;:\;\; J_{w-\Oh}\,g(\nu)=\int_{-\eta}^{\eta}e^{wu}\,g\!\big(e^{u}\nu\big)\,\dd u , \]

which applies each closed form to an arbitrary spectrum \(g(\nu)\) (blackbody included), not just to power laws. Power laws are the eigenfunctions that make this concrete: since \(\Oh\,\nu^{q}=-q\,\nu^{q}\), any function of the generator obeys \(f(\Oh)\,\nu^{q}=f(-q)\,\nu^{q}\) (L1), and in particular \(J_{w-\Oh}\,\nu^{q}=J_{w+q}\,\nu^{q}\). Hence

\[ \boxed{\;\text{operator form}\;\xrightarrow[\;\text{on }\nu^{q}\;]{\;\Oh\,\to\,-q\;}\;\text{power-law form}, \qquad J_{w-\Oh}\;\longleftrightarrow\;J_{w+q}.\;} \]

The dictionary is faithful — the eigenvalues on all \(\nu^q\) determine the operator completely — so nothing is lost either way; the \(\gamma,p\) coefficients carry no \(\Oh\) at all, and every factor below is a function of \(\Oh\) alone, so all factors commute and no ordering ambiguity arises. The seed reproduces the published monopole closed form exactly,

\[ \Do_{000}=\frac{J_{1-\Oh}\,J_{2-\Oh}}{4\gamma p^{2}} =\frac{(\gamma+p)^{3-2\Oh}+(\gamma-p)^{3-2\Oh}-2\gamma}{4(2-\Oh)(1-\Oh)\,\gamma\,p^{2}} \]

[CR26, Eq. 10a] — note the denominators \((2-\Oh)(1-\Oh)\) are exactly the \(w\) of the two brackets \(J_{w-\Oh}\).

Form of the entries — a scalar times two brackets all 216 factor; dev 0

The rank-one structure of (P2) makes every element bilinear in the brackets, so each entry factors into a scalar prefactor times exactly two brackets linear in \(J\), \(\Do_{\ell\ell'\ell''}=S(\gamma,p)\,B_1(\Oh)\,B_2(\Oh)\) — the shape in which the literature reports the few known cases. The factorization exists only modulo \(\gamma^2=1+p^2\); it is found by factoring in the rational \(X=e^{\eta}\) representation, where that relation is automatic. It reproduces the published expressions exactly, e.g.

\[ \Do_{101}=\tfrac{3}{4\gamma p^4}\big(\gamma J_{1-\Oh}-J_{-\Oh}\big)\big(\gamma J_{2-\Oh}-J_{3-\Oh}\big),\qquad \Do_{121}=\tfrac{15}{16\gamma p^{8}}\,W(\Oh)\,W(3-\Oh). \]
Conventions (as in the foundational papers) RC26, Eq. 9; CR26, Eqs. 10a, 14

The Doppler operator is the round trip \(\Do^m_{\ell\ell'\ell''}(\nu,\beta)=\gamma^{-1}\,{}^{-1}\Bo^m_{\ell\ell'}(\nu,-\beta)\,{}^{0}\Bo^m_{\ell'\ell''}(\nu,\beta)\) [RC26, Eq. 9], here the scalar \(m=0\) sector. With \(\gamma=\cosh\eta\), \(p=\sinh\eta=\gamma\beta\), the bracket is \(J_{w}=2\sinh(w\eta)/w=\big[(\gamma{+}p)^{w}-(\gamma{-}p)^{w}\big]/w\), promoted to the operator \(J_{w-\Oh}\) above. The diffusion operator of the papers is \(\Dv=\Oh^2-3\Oh\) [CR26, Eq. 14].

These are exact closed forms, not \(p\)-series — valid at every speed, with nothing truncated (verified against the defining kernel integrals to deviation \(0\), out to \(\beta=0.999\)).
