Closed-form catalog of the Doppler operator — generated by the pure-D ladder

Every Doppler operator \(\Do^0_{\ell\ell'\ell''}(\nu,\beta)\) up to \(\Do_{555}\) below is produced from the single seed \(\Do_{000}\) by the recurrence ladder of the Doppler operator itself — the outer axis ladder (P0), the middle-index linear recurrence (P3′), reflection (N1) and the rank-one fill (P2). The aberration kernel is never used to generate anything; it enters only as an independent numeric check. Every element reduces to the border tower \(\Do_{\ell\ell'0}\) through

\[ \Do_{\ell\ell'\ell''}(q)=\frac{\Do_{\ell\ell'0}(q)\,\Do_{\ell''\ell'0}(-3-q)}{\Do_{0\ell'0}(q)}\qquad\text{(P2 + N1)}, \]

and the border tower is climbed from \(\Do_{000}\) by P0 (raise \(\ell\)) and P3′ (raise \(\ell'\)).

Form of the entries — a scalar times two brackets all 216 factor; dev 0

The rank-one structure of (P2) makes every element bilinear in the brackets, so each entry factors into a scalar prefactor times exactly two linear-in-\(J\) brackets, \(\Do_{\ell\ell'\ell''}(q)=S(\gamma,p)\,B_1(J)\,B_2(J)\) — the same shape in which the literature reports the few known cases. This factorization exists only modulo \(\gamma^2=1+p^2\); it is found by factoring in the rational \(X=e^{\eta}\) representation, where that relation is automatic. Reassuringly, it reproduces the published expressions exactly, e.g.

\[ \Do_{101}(q)=\tfrac{3}{4\gamma p^4}\big(\gamma J_{q+1}-J_q\big)\big(\gamma J_{q+2}-J_{q+3}\big),\qquad \Do_{121}(q)=\tfrac{15}{16\gamma p^{8}}\,W(q)\,W(-3-q). \]
Conventions (as in the foundational papers) RC26, Eq. 9; CR26, Eqs. 10a, 14

The Doppler operator is the round trip \(\Do^m_{\ell\ell'\ell''}(\nu,\beta)=\gamma^{-1}\,{}^{-1}\Bo^m_{\ell\ell'}(\nu,-\beta)\,{}^{0}\Bo^m_{\ell'\ell''}(\nu,\beta)\) [RC26, Eq. 9], here the scalar \(m=0\) sector. Its entries are functions of the energy-shift generator \(\Oh=-\nu\partial_\nu\); we tabulate the eigenvalue on a power law, \(\Do(q)\) with \(\Oh\,\nu^{q}=-q\,\nu^{q}\) (so \(q=0\) is the CMB blackbody slope). With \(\gamma=\cosh\eta\), \(p=\sinh\eta=\gamma\beta\) and \(J_{q+k}\equiv 2\sinh\!\big((q{+}k)\eta\big)/(q{+}k)=\big[(\gamma{+}p)^{q+k}-(\gamma{-}p)^{q+k}\big]/(q{+}k)\), the seed matches the paper's monopole closed form exactly: \(\Do_{000}(q)=\tfrac{J_{q+1}J_{q+2}}{4\gamma p^2} =\tfrac{(\gamma+p)^{3+2q}+(\gamma-p)^{3+2q}-2\gamma}{4(1+q)(2+q)\gamma p^2}\) [CR26, Eq. 10a, \(\Oh\to-q\)].

These are exact closed forms, not \(p\)-series — valid at every speed, with nothing truncated (verified against the defining kernel integrals to deviation \(0\), out to \(\beta=0.99\)). All 216 are machine-generated by the pure-D ladder (harness 40); the machine-readable set is data/closed_forms_pureD.json.
