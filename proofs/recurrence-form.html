Recurrence form — every operator in terms of other Doppler operators

The closed-form catalog gives each element as an explicit function of \(\gamma,p\) and the brackets \(J_{q+k}\). This page gives the complementary presentation asked of a recurrence scheme: each operator written in terms of other Doppler operators, one index down the ladder. Every symbol on a right-hand side is a physical \(\Do\); the only scalars are \(\gamma\), \(p\), the Legendre constants \(C_\ell=\ell/\sqrt{4\ell^2-1}\), and elementary bracket ratios.

Everything the generation needs — nothing else all 216 from the seed, dev 0 (harness 45)

The generation is self-contained. The complete input is:

\[ \gamma=\cosh\eta,\qquad p=\sinh\eta,\qquad J_w=\frac{2\sinh(w\eta)}{w},\qquad C_\ell=\frac{\ell}{\sqrt{4\ell^2-1}},\qquad \Do_{000}(q)=\frac{J_{q+1}J_{q+2}}{4\gamma p^{2}} , \]

with the constants completely explicit, \(C_1=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt3},\;C_2=\tfrac{2}{\sqrt{15}},\;C_3=\tfrac{3}{\sqrt{35}},\; C_4=\tfrac{4}{3\sqrt7},\;C_5=\tfrac{5}{3\sqrt{11}}\), plus the four relations below. No auxiliary coefficient family is required at any point — every symbol on a right-hand side is either one of the elementary scalars above or a Doppler operator already obtained at a lower index. Starting from \(\Do_{000}\) alone, this reproduces all 216 elements — \(\Do_{555}\) included — to deviation 0 against the aberration-kernel definition.

The three one-step rules — complete coverage of all 216 elements each verified dev 0 (harness 43)

Exactly one rule applies to each element, by index type:

\[ \textbf{(P2+N1)}\quad \Do_{\ell\ell'\ell''}(q)=\frac{\Do_{\ell\ell'0}(q)\;\Do_{\ell''\ell'0}(-3-q)}{\Do_{0\ell'0}(q)} \qquad(\ell''>0) \] \[ \textbf{(P3)}\quad \Do_{h\ell'0}(q)=\frac{1}{C_{\ell'}}\Bigg[\frac{\Do_{\ell'00}(q{-}1)}{p} \left(\gamma\frac{\Do_{h,\ell'-1,0}(q)}{\Do_{\ell'-1,00}(q{-}1)} -\frac{J_{q-1}}{J_q}\frac{\Do_{h,\ell'-1,0}(q{-}1)}{\Do_{\ell'-1,00}(q{-}2)}\right) -C_{\ell'-1}\frac{\Do_{\ell'00}(q{-}1)}{\Do_{\ell'-2,00}(q{-}1)}\Do_{h,\ell'-2,0}(q)\Bigg] \qquad(\ell''=0,\ \ell'\ge1) \] \[ \textbf{(P0)}\quad \Do_{a00}(q)=\frac{1}{C_a}\left[\frac{\gamma}{p}\Do_{a-1,00}(q) -\frac{1}{p}\frac{J_{q+1}}{J_{q+2}}\Do_{a-1,00}(q{+}1)-C_{a-1}\Do_{a-2,00}(q)\right] \qquad(\ell''=\ell'=0,\ a\ge1) \]

with the single seed \(\Do_{000}(q)=J_{q+1}J_{q+2}/(4\gamma p^2)\). Coverage: 180 elements by (P2+N1), 30 by (P3), 5 by (P0), plus the seed — 216 in total, each checked against the aberration-kernel definition to deviation 0.

Worked example — \(\Do_{555}\) all the way down

Applying the rules repeatedly walks any element down to the seed. For the top element:

\[ \begin{aligned} \Do_{555}(q)&=\frac{\Do_{550}(q)\,\Do_{550}(-3-q)}{\Do_{050}(q)} &&\text{(P2+N1)}\\[4pt] \Do_{5\ell'0}&\;\longleftarrow\;\Do_{5,\ell'-1,0},\ \Do_{5,\ell'-2,0}\ \text{and axis }\Do_{a00} &&\text{(P3), }\ \ell'=5,4,3,2,1\\[4pt] \Do_{500}&\;\longleftarrow\;\Do_{400},\ \Do_{300} &&\text{(P0)}\\[4pt] &\;\;\vdots\;\longrightarrow\;\Do_{000} &&\text{(seed)} \end{aligned} \]

so \(\Do_{555}\) is reached from \(\Do_{000}\) by five (P0) steps along the axis, five (P3) steps in the middle index, and one (P2+N1) fill — never leaving the physical family.

Fully substituted — \(\Do_{555}\) in terms of \(\Do_{000}\) alone

Applying the relations repeatedly and back-substituting eliminates every intermediate operator. Unrolling (P0) and then (P3) writes each border element as a finite combination of the seed at integer-shifted spectral index,

\[ \Do_{h\ell'0}(q)=\sum_{m=-\ell'}^{h}c^{(h,\ell')}_m(q)\,\Do_{000}(q+m), \tag{E} \]

with \(c\) elementary in \(\gamma,p\) and the brackets \(J_w\). The step that closes the argument is that the seed is reflection-symmetric — (N1) at zero indices gives \(\Do_{000}(-3-q)=\Do_{000}(q)\) — so the reflected factor in (P2) folds back onto the same set of shifts, \(\Do_{h\ell'0}(-3-q)=\sum_m c^{(h,\ell')}_m(-3-q)\,\Do_{000}(q-m)\). Hence

\[ \boxed{\;\Do_{555}(q)= \frac{\Big[\sum_{m=-5}^{5}c^{(5,5)}_m(q)\,\Do_{000}(q+m)\Big]\; \Big[\sum_{m=-5}^{5}c^{(5,5)}_m(-3-q)\,\Do_{000}(q-m)\Big]} {\sum_{m=-5}^{0}c^{(0,5)}_m(q)\,\Do_{000}(q+m)}\;} \]

— the top element written entirely in terms of the lowest operator, \(\Do_{000}\), evaluated at \(q,\,q\pm1,\dots,q\pm5\). The numerator brackets carry \(11\) seed terms each, the denominator \(6\).

The lowest cases show the pattern, and reproduce the boxed relations of the recurrences page exactly:

\[ \begin{aligned} \Do_{100}(q)&=\frac{\sqrt3\,\gamma}{p}\,\Do_{000}(q)-\frac{\sqrt3}{p}\frac{J_{q+1}}{J_{q+2}}\,\Do_{000}(q{+}1),\\[4pt] \Do_{010}(q)&=\frac{3\big(\gamma J_{q+1}-J_{q+2}\big)}{p^{2}} \left[\frac{\gamma}{J_{q+1}}\Do_{000}(q)-\frac{1}{J_{q}}\Do_{000}(q{-}1)\right]. \end{aligned} \]

The coefficients, written out — \(\Do_{550}\)

For \(\Do_{550}\) the eleven coefficients are, completely explicitly,

\[ c_m(q)=\frac{\sqrt{11}}{512\,p^{15}}\;\frac{b(q)\,a_m}{J_{q+1+m}},\qquad m=-5,\dots,5, \]

with a single \(q\)-dependent bracket shared by all of them,

\[ b(q)=\gamma(63{+}56p^2{+}8p^4)J_{q+1}-15(21{+}28p^2{+}8p^4)J_{q+2}+210\gamma(3{+}2p^2)J_{q+3} -70(9{+}8p^2)J_{q+4}+315\gamma J_{q+5}-63J_{q+6}, \]

and eleven pure \(\gamma,p\) scalars which are symmetric, \(a_m=a_{-m}\), so only six are distinct:

\[ \begin{aligned} a_{0}&=22\big(500094+1003275p^2+641200p^4+141120p^6+7680p^8+32p^{10}\big),\\ a_{\pm1}&=-330\,\gamma\big(27783+41160p^2+17360p^4+2016p^6+32p^8\big),\\ a_{\pm2}&=4620\big(1134+2163p^2+1270p^4+236p^6+8p^8\big),\\ a_{\pm3}&=-385\,\gamma\big(5103+6552p^2+2120p^4+128p^6\big),\\ a_{\pm4}&=3465\big(126+203p^2+88p^4+8p^6\big),\\ a_{\pm5}&=-693\,\gamma\big(63+56p^2+8p^4\big). \end{aligned} \]

These are exact, not expansions: each \(a_m\) is a polynomial of finite degree that terminates — there is no discarded tail. Typed out literally and evaluated against the defining kernel integrals, the formula holds to quadrature precision at every speed tested, including \(\beta=0.9999\) (\(p=70.7\)), where a series truncated at \(p^{10}\) would be wrong by many orders of magnitude:

\(\beta\)0.420.900.990.9990.9999

The \(\gamma\) appears only for odd \(|m|\) — the parity law (N2) showing through — and \(a_m=a_{-m}\) is why the eleven coefficients collapse to six.

Numerical caution. \(b(q)\) is common to every term and factors out of the sum, so the only \(m\)-dependence is \(a_m/J_{q+1+m}\). The \(p^{-15}\) prefactor against these large integers means severe cancellation at small \(\beta\): the expression is exact but ill-conditioned there, and the recurrence form above is the better route for numerical evaluation.

harness 46: expansion (E) exact for \((h,\ell')\) up to \((5,5)\); seed reflection symmetry; \(\Do_{555}\) assembled from seed shifts equals the direct product form (symbolic); and matches the aberration-kernel definition to deviation 0
