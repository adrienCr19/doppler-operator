Recurrence form — every operator in terms of other Doppler operators

The closed-form catalog gives each element as an explicit function of \(\gamma,p\) and the brackets \(J_{q+k}\). This page gives the complementary presentation asked of a recurrence scheme: each operator written in terms of other Doppler operators, one index down the ladder. Every symbol on a right-hand side is a physical \(\Do\); the only scalars are \(\gamma\), \(p\), the Legendre constants \(C_\ell=\ell/\sqrt{4\ell^2-1}\), and elementary bracket ratios.

The three one-step rules — complete coverage of all 216 elements each verified dev 0 (harness 43)

Exactly one rule applies to each element, by index type:

\[ \textbf{(P2+N1)}\quad \Do_{\ell\ell'\ell''}(q)=\frac{\Do_{\ell\ell'0}(q)\;\Do_{\ell''\ell'0}(-3-q)}{\Do_{0\ell'0}(q)} \qquad(\ell''>0) \] \[ \textbf{(P3)}\quad \Do_{h\ell'0}(q)=\frac{1}{C_{\ell'}}\Bigg[\frac{\Do_{\ell'00}(q{-}1)}{p} \left(\gamma\frac{\Do_{h,\ell'-1,0}(q)}{\Do_{\ell'-1,00}(q{-}1)} -\frac{J_{q-1}}{J_q}\frac{\Do_{h,\ell'-1,0}(q{-}1)}{\Do_{\ell'-1,00}(q{-}2)}\right) -C_{\ell'-1}\frac{\Do_{\ell'00}(q{-}1)}{\Do_{\ell'-2,00}(q{-}1)}\Do_{h,\ell'-2,0}(q)\Bigg] \qquad(\ell''=0,\ \ell'\ge1) \] \[ \textbf{(P0)}\quad \Do_{a00}(q)=\frac{1}{C_a}\left[\frac{\gamma}{p}\Do_{a-1,00}(q) -\frac{1}{p}\frac{J_{q+1}}{J_{q+2}}\Do_{a-1,00}(q{+}1)-C_{a-1}\Do_{a-2,00}(q)\right] \qquad(\ell''=\ell'=0,\ a\ge1) \]

with the single seed \(\Do_{000}(q)=J_{q+1}J_{q+2}/(4\gamma p^2)\). Coverage: 180 elements by (P2+N1), 30 by (P3), 5 by (P0), plus the seed — 216 in total, each checked against the aberration-kernel definition to deviation 0.

Worked example — \(\Do_{555}\) all the way down

Applying the rules repeatedly walks any element down to the seed. For the top element:

\[ \begin{aligned} \Do_{555}(q)&=\frac{\Do_{550}(q)\,\Do_{550}(-3-q)}{\Do_{050}(q)} &&\text{(P2+N1)}\\[4pt] \Do_{5\ell'0}&\;\longleftarrow\;\Do_{5,\ell'-1,0},\ \Do_{5,\ell'-2,0}\ \text{and axis }\Do_{a00} &&\text{(P3), }\ \ell'=5,4,3,2,1\\[4pt] \Do_{500}&\;\longleftarrow\;\Do_{400},\ \Do_{300} &&\text{(P0)}\\[4pt] &\;\;\vdots\;\longrightarrow\;\Do_{000} &&\text{(seed)} \end{aligned} \]

so \(\Do_{555}\) is reached from \(\Do_{000}\) by five (P0) steps along the axis, five (P3) steps in the middle index, and one (P2+N1) fill — never leaving the physical family.

Reduction to a fundamental basis

Unrolling the middle rule (the resummation, proved by induction in §4.3) collapses the \(\ell'\)-dependence entirely, leaving only the axis family \(\Do_{a00}\) evaluated at integer-shifted spectral index:

\[ \Do_{h\ell'0}(q)=\mathcal J_{\ell'}(q-1)\sum_{j=0}^{\ell'}U^{(\ell')}_j\,\frac{\Do_{h00}(q-j)}{J_{q+1}}, \qquad \Do_{\ell\ell'\ell''}(q)=\frac{\Do_{\ell\ell'0}(q)\,\Do_{\ell''\ell'0}(-3-q)}{\Do_{0\ell'0}(q)} . \]

So the basis is one-index: \(\{\Do_{000},\Do_{100},\Do_{200},\dots\}\) (equivalently, by reflection (N1), \(\{\Do_{00a}\}\)). The outer indices \(\ell,\ell''\) enter only through \(\Do_{\ell00}\) and \(\Do_{\ell''00}\); the middle index \(\ell'\) enters only through the elementary weights \(U^{(\ell')}_j\) acting as spectral shifts. The axis family is in turn generated from the single seed \(\Do_{000}\) by (P0), so \(\Do_{000}\) alone generates everything — though the axis elements are not themselves finite elementary combinations of spectral shifts of \(\Do_{000}\), which is why the axis tower is the natural place for the reduction to bottom out.
