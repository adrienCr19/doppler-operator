The exact recurrence relations of the Doppler operator

Complete proofs, carried to conclusion, from the aberration kernel. This page is the core of the project. Every central result below is a relation among the physical Doppler operators \(\Do_{\ell\ell'\ell''}(q)\) of the literature — no extended weight lattice, no off-diagonal family. The aberration kernel of the boost operator appears only inside the proofs, as the object \(\Do\) is defined from. The method (§§3–5) is uniform: state the claim among Doppler operators, replace every operator by its aberration-kernel product, and reduce algebraically — using the four kernel lemmas established from the integral in §2 — until the two sides coincide, cancelling every auxiliary object along the way. Each kernel lemma is verified to machine zero in 36, and each \(\Do\)-identity's reduction \(\text{LHS}-\text{RHS}\to0\) is carried out symbolically (kernels as abstract symbols) in 37; numerics in 34 and 35.

The recurrence relations (physical Doppler operator only) central result verified, dev = 0

With \(C_\ell=\ell/\sqrt{4\ell^2-1}\), \(\gamma=\cosh\eta\), \(p=\sinh\eta\), \(J_w=\frac{(\gamma+p)^w-(\gamma-p)^w}{w}=\frac{2\sinh(w\eta)}{w}\), and the seed \(\Do_{000}(q)=\frac{J_{2+q}J_{1+q}}{4\gamma p^2}\), the whole catalogue is generated from \(\Do_{000}\) by these four relations — each term a physical \(\Do\):

\[ \textbf{(P0) raise an outer index (on the axis):}\quad C_a\,\Do_{a00}(q) = \frac\gamma p\Do_{a-1,00}(q) - \frac1p\,\frac{J_{1+q}}{J_{2+q}}\,\Do_{a-1,00}(q{+}1) - C_{a-1}\Do_{a-2,00}(q), \] \[ \textbf{(P3) raise the middle index:}\quad C_{\ell'+1}\Do_{h,\ell'+1,0}(q) = \frac{\Do_{\ell'+1,00}(q{-}1)}{p}\Big[\gamma\tfrac{\Do_{h\ell'0}(q)}{\Do_{\ell'00}(q{-}1)} - \tfrac{J_{q-1}}{J_q}\tfrac{\Do_{h\ell'0}(q{-}1)}{\Do_{\ell'00}(q{-}2)}\Big] - C_{\ell'}\tfrac{\Do_{\ell'+1,00}(q{-}1)}{\Do_{\ell'-1,00}(q{-}1)}\Do_{h,\ell'-1,0}(q), \] \[ \textbf{(N1) reflection:}\quad \Do_{h\ell'l}(q)=\Do_{l\ell'h}(-3-q), \qquad \textbf{(P2) rank-one fill:}\quad \Do_{h\ell'l}(q)=\frac{\Do_{h\ell'0}(q)\,\Do_{0\ell'l}(q)}{\Do_{0\ell'0}(q)}. \]

Recipe. P0 climbs the two outer axes from \(\Do_{000}\); P3 climbs the middle index up each \(\ell''{=}0\) column to the border \(\Do_{h\ell'0}\); N1 turns those into the \(\ell{=}0\) border; P2 fills the interior. Every intermediate is a physical Doppler operator. For the \(p\)-series there is also the rapidity ODE (N3), which likewise closes on physical operators at fixed \(\ell'\).

P3 looks nonlinear (ratios of \(\Do\)), but §4.1 shows those ratios are only ever axis elements — which are closed in \(J_w\) — so P3 is in fact a linear \(\ell'\)-recurrence with explicit elementary coefficients, given there for general \(\ell'\). The genuine obstructions are sharper: a fixed-\(q\) middle recurrence does not exist (the shift \(q\to q{-}1\) is essential), and off the axis the outer/inner raise is not elementary — both verified in harness 39.

The rest of this page proves each of these from the aberration kernel: §1 the reduction; §2 the four kernel identities; §3 the derivation of P0; §4 of P3; §5 of N1 and P2.

1  The two objects and the reduction

The aberration kernel of the boost operator, at scalar weight \(d\) and \(s=0\), is the matrix element (\(\bar Y_\ell(\mu)=\sqrt{(2\ell+1)/4\pi}\,P_\ell(\mu)\)):

\[ {}^{d}\Kk_{\ell\ell'}(-\beta) \;=\; 2\pi\!\int_{-1}^{1} \bar Y_\ell(\mu)\,\bar Y_{\ell'}(\mu')\,\big[\gamma(1+\beta\mu)\big]^{-d}\,\dd\mu, \qquad \mu' = \frac{\mu+\beta}{1+\beta\mu}, \tag{1.1} \]

and \({}^{d}\Kk_{\ell\ell'}(+\beta)\) is the same with \(\beta\to-\beta\). The Doppler operator is the physically assembled round trip \(\Do^m_{\ell\ell'\ell''}=\gamma^{-1}\,{}^{-1}\Bo^m_{\ell\ell'}(\nu,-\beta)\,{}^{0}\Bo^m_{\ell'\ell''}(\nu,\beta)\); on \(\nu^q\) the boost operators reduce to kernels (Lemma L1: \(\Oh\to-q\)), giving the product that everything below rests on:

\[ \Do_{\ell\ell'\ell''}(q) \;=\; \frac{1}{\gamma}\;{}^{-1-q}\Kk_{\ell\ell'}(+\beta)\;{}^{-q}\Kk_{\ell'\ell''}(-\beta). \tag{1.2} \]

The outgoing index \(\ell\) is the row of factor 1; the incoming index \(\ell''\) is the column of factor 2; the scattering index \(\ell'\) is shared — the column of factor 1 and the row of factor 2. Factor 2 on the axis is the seed alone, \({}^{-q}\Kk_{00}(-\beta)=J_{1+q}/2p\) (§2.1), which is exactly what lets the outer ladder close on physical operators (§3). The single spectral shift \(q\to q\pm1\) that appears in P0/P3 keeps every object a physical Doppler operator.

2  The four kernel identities, proved from the integral

Two elementary aberration facts, used repeatedly. From \(\mu'=(\mu+\beta)/(1+\beta\mu)\),

\[ 1+\beta\mu = \frac{1}{\gamma^2(1-\beta\mu')}, \qquad \frac{\dd\mu}{\dd\mu'} = \frac{1-\beta^2}{(1-\beta\mu')^2}, \qquad \gamma(1\pm\beta)=\gamma\pm p=e^{\pm\eta}. \tag{2.0} \]

2.1  Seed (the one integral)

With \(\bar Y_0=1/\sqrt{4\pi}\),

\[ {}^{d}\Kk_{00}(-\beta) = \frac{\gamma^{-d}}{2\beta}\,\frac{(1+\beta)^{1-d}-(1-\beta)^{1-d}}{1-d} = \frac{1}{2p(1-d)}\big[(\gamma+p)^{1-d}-(\gamma-p)^{1-d}\big], \]

using \(\gamma^{-d}(1\pm\beta)^{1-d}=\gamma^{-1}(\gamma\pm p)^{1-d}\) and \(\gamma^{-1}/\beta=1/p\). Hence

\[ \boxed{\;{}^{d}\Kk_{00}(\pm\beta) = \frac{J_{1-d}}{2p}\;} \tag{2.1} \]

I1, dev 0

2.2  Parity

Substituting \(\mu\to-\mu\) in (1.1) sends \(\bar Y_\ell(-\mu)=(-1)^\ell\bar Y_\ell\), turns the weight into the \(+\beta\) weight, and the aberrated argument into minus the \(+\beta\) aberration (a further \((-1)^{\ell'}\)):

\[ \boxed{\;{}^{d}\Kk_{\ell\ell'}(-\beta) = (-1)^{\ell+\ell'}\,{}^{d}\Kk_{\ell\ell'}(+\beta)\;} \tag{2.2} \]

I2, dev 0

2.3  Transposition

Change the integration variable in (1.1) to \(\mu'\). By (2.0) the weight times the Jacobian is \([\gamma(1-\beta\mu')]^{d-2}\), the two harmonics swap roles, and \(\mu(\mu')\) is the \(+\beta\) aberration of \(\mu'\):

\[ \boxed{\;{}^{d}\Kk_{\ell\ell'}(-\beta) = {}^{2-d}\Kk_{\ell'\ell}(+\beta)\;} \tag{2.3} \]

I3, dev 0

2.4  The master \(\ell\)-raising recurrence

Two ingredients. The Legendre relation (matrix element of \(\mu\)):

\[ \mu\,\bar Y_\ell = C_{\ell+1}\,\bar Y_{\ell+1} + C_\ell\,\bar Y_{\ell-1}, \qquad C_\ell=\frac{\ell}{\sqrt{4\ell^2-1}}, \tag{2.4a} \]

(I0, dev 0), and the \(\mu\)-to-weight identity, from \(\mu=\frac1\beta[(1+\beta\mu)-1]\) and \((1+\beta\mu)[\gamma(1+\beta\mu)]^{-d}=\gamma^{-1}[\gamma(1+\beta\mu)]^{-(d-1)}\):

\[ \mu\,\big[\gamma(1+\beta\mu)\big]^{-d} = \frac1\beta\Big[\gamma^{-1}\big[\gamma(1+\beta\mu)\big]^{-(d-1)} - \big[\gamma(1+\beta\mu)\big]^{-d}\Big] \tag{2.4b} \]

(I4a, dev 0). Insert (2.4a) under the integral; the extra \(\mu\) multiplies the weight, and (2.4b) turns it into two kernels of adjacent weight. Equating the two evaluations of \(2\pi\!\int(\mu\bar Y_\ell)\bar Y_{\ell'}(\mu')\,[\gamma(1+\beta\mu)]^{-d}\dd\mu\) and shifting \(\ell+1\to\ell\):

\[ \boxed{\;C_\ell\,{}^{d}\Kk_{\ell\ell'}(-\beta) = \frac1p\,{}^{d-1}\Kk_{\ell-1,\ell'}(-\beta) - \frac{\gamma}{p}\,{}^{d}\Kk_{\ell-1,\ell'}(-\beta) - C_{\ell-1}\,{}^{d}\Kk_{\ell-2,\ell'}(-\beta)\;} \tag{KR} \]

I4, dev 0. The \(+\beta\) form follows by multiplying through by \((-1)^{\ell+\ell'}\) and applying parity (2.2) term by term (the \((\ell-1)\)-terms flip sign):

\[ {}^{d}\Kk_{\ell\ell'}(+\beta) = \frac{1}{C_\ell p}\Big[\gamma\,{}^{d}\Kk_{\ell-1,\ell'}(+\beta) - {}^{d-1}\Kk_{\ell-1,\ell'}(+\beta)\Big] - \frac{C_{\ell-1}}{C_\ell}\,{}^{d}\Kk_{\ell-2,\ell'}(+\beta). \tag{KR$^+$} \]

I4+, dev 0. These four identities — seed, parity, transposition, master recurrence — are everything the Doppler recurrences are built from.

Method for §§3–5. Each identity is proved the same way: state the claim among Doppler operators, replace every operator by its kernel product (1.2), and reduce — using only the four lemmas of §2 — until the two sides coincide. Every reduction is also carried out symbolically, with the kernels as abstract commuting symbols, in 37 (each claim's \(\text{LHS}-\text{RHS}\to0\)).

3  Raising an outer index — P0

Claim.

\[ \boxed{\;C_a\,\Do_{a00}(q) = \frac{\gamma}{p}\,\Do_{a-1,00}(q) - \frac{1}{p}\,\frac{J_{1+q}}{J_{2+q}}\,\Do_{a-1,00}(q{+}1) - C_{a-1}\,\Do_{a-2,00}(q)\;} \tag{P0} \]

Proof. Write each operator by (1.2). Every second factor here is the monopole \({}^{-q}\Kk_{00}(-\beta)\), which the seed (2.1) evaluates, so each axis element is a single kernel times a scalar:

\[ \Do_{a00}(q) = \frac{J_{1+q}}{2\gamma p}\,{}^{-1-q}\Kk_{a0}(+\beta), \qquad \Do_{a-1,00}(q{+}1) = \frac{J_{2+q}}{2\gamma p}\,{}^{-2-q}\Kk_{a-1,0}(+\beta) \]

(the second uses the seed at \(q{+}1\): \({}^{-(q+1)}\Kk_{00}(-\beta)=J_{2+q}/2p\), and weight \(-1-(q{+}1)=-2-q\)). Substitute these into the claim. The scalar \(J_{1+q}/2\gamma p\) is common to every term — in the \(\Do_{a-1,00}(q{+}1)\) term the factor \(\tfrac{J_{1+q}}{J_{2+q}}\cdot\tfrac{J_{2+q}}{2\gamma p}=\tfrac{J_{1+q}}{2\gamma p}\) — and divides out, leaving exactly

\[ C_a\,{}^{-1-q}\Kk_{a0}(+\beta) = \frac{\gamma}{p}\,{}^{-1-q}\Kk_{a-1,0}(+\beta) - \frac{1}{p}\,{}^{-2-q}\Kk_{a-1,0}(+\beta) - C_{a-1}\,{}^{-1-q}\Kk_{a-2,0}(+\beta), \]

which is the master recurrence (KR\(^+\)) at weight \(d=-1-q\), row \(a\), column \(0\) (multiply (KR\(^+\)) by \(C_a\); note \({}^{d-1}\Kk={}^{-2-q}\Kk\)). Since (KR\(^+\)) holds (§2.4), the claim holds. \(\blacksquare\) harness 37, P0 → 0; harness 34, P0a/P0b, dev 0

The single weight-shifted companion of the kernel step has become the physical operator \(\Do_{a-1,00}(q{+}1)\) precisely because factor 2 is the seed. Off the axis factor 2 is no longer the seed and the companion is not a single physical element; the physical route is then the rank-one fill P2 (§5) after raising the middle index, and the kernel-level weight-lattice relations N5/N6 are scaffolding, confined to the N5/N6 page.

4  Raising the middle index — P3

Claim.

\[ \boxed{ \begin{aligned} C_{\ell'+1}\,\Do_{h,\ell'+1,0}(q) =\;& \frac{\Do_{\ell'+1,00}(q{-}1)}{p} \Big[\gamma\,\tfrac{\Do_{h\ell'0}(q)}{\Do_{\ell'00}(q{-}1)} - \tfrac{J_{q-1}}{J_q}\,\tfrac{\Do_{h\ell'0}(q{-}1)}{\Do_{\ell'00}(q{-}2)}\Big] - C_{\ell'}\,\tfrac{\Do_{\ell'+1,00}(q{-}1)}{\Do_{\ell'-1,00}(q{-}1)}\,\Do_{h,\ell'-1,0}(q). \end{aligned}} \tag{P3} \]

Proof. Write every operator by (1.2), and reduce each second factor by the seed (2.1) and parity (2.2). The key is that each \(\Do\)-ratio in the claim then collapses to a single first-factor kernel — the dictionary

\[ \frac{\Do_{h\ell'0}(q)}{\Do_{\ell'00}(q-1)} = (-1)^{\ell'}\frac{2p}{J_q}\,{}^{-1-q}\Kk_{h\ell'}(+\beta), \tag{D} \]

which follows from \(\Do_{h\ell'0}(q)=\gamma^{-1}{}^{-1-q}\Kk_{h\ell'}(+\beta)\,{}^{-q}\Kk_{\ell'0}(-\beta)\), \({}^{-q}\Kk_{\ell'0}(-\beta)=(-1)^{\ell'}{}^{-q}\Kk_{\ell'0}(+\beta)\) (parity), and \(\Do_{\ell'00}(q-1)=\gamma^{-1}{}^{-q}\Kk_{\ell'0}(+\beta)\cdot J_q/2p\) (seed), so the \({}^{-q}\Kk_{\ell'0}(+\beta)\) cancels. Substituting (D) and its \(q{-}1\) analogue, and the seed-reduced axis coefficients \(\Do_{\ell'\pm1,00}(q{-}1)\), into the claim and cancelling the common \((-1)^{\ell'}J_q/2p\) and the axis kernels leaves exactly the column recurrence (A) for the raised kernel,

\[ {}^{-1-q}\Kk_{h,\ell'+1}(+\beta) = -\frac{1}{C_{\ell'+1} p}\Big[\gamma\,{}^{-1-q}\Kk_{h,\ell'}(+\beta) - {}^{-q}\Kk_{h,\ell'}(+\beta)\Big] - \frac{C_{\ell'}}{C_{\ell'+1}}\,{}^{-1-q}\Kk_{h,\ell'-1}(+\beta), \tag{A} \]

which is (KR) transposed (2.3), raising the shared column \(\ell'\) of factor 1 (R-A, dev 0). Since (A) holds, the claim holds. \(\blacksquare\) harness 37, P3 → 0; harness 34, P3, dev 1.3×10⁻³⁷

4.1  The coefficients are elementary — P3 is a linear recurrence in \(J_w\)

A refinement, not a requirement. For generating operators, use (P3) exactly as boxed above: its coefficients are ratios of axis elements \(\Do_{a00}\), which the ladder has already produced, so nothing further is needed. This subsection shows in addition that those ratios are elementary — which is worth knowing structurally — at the cost of introducing the bracket \(\mathcal J_a\). If you only want the operators, skip to §5.

The ratios in (P3) are not a genuine nonlinearity. Every Doppler operator inside the coefficients is an axis element \(\Do_{a00}\), and these are closed. Writing \(P_a\) for the Legendre polynomial and

\[ \mathcal J_a(q) := \gamma^{1+q}\!\int_{-1}^1 P_a(\mu)\,(1-\beta\mu)^{1+q}\,\mathrm d\mu = (-1)^a\sum_{n=0}^{a} [\mu^n]P_a\; T_n(1{+}q), \qquad T_n(s)=\frac{1}{p^{\,n+1}}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}(-\gamma)^k\,J_{s+n+1-k}, \tag{J} \]

a finite combination of the brackets \(J_w=2\sinh(w\eta)/w\), the axis elements and their first instances are

\[ \Do_{a00}(q)=\frac{\sqrt{2a+1}}{4\gamma p}\,J_{1+q}\,\mathcal J_a(q),\qquad \mathcal J_0=\frac{J_{2+q}}{p},\;\; \mathcal J_1=\frac{\gamma J_{2+q}-J_{3+q}}{p^2},\;\; \mathcal J_2=\frac{3J_{4+q}-6\gamma J_{3+q}+(2\gamma^2{+}1)J_{2+q}}{2p^3}. \]

Substituting these into (P3) — the \(\sqrt{2\ell'{+}1}\) and \(C_{\ell'}\) prefactors all collapse to rationals — turns it into a manifestly linear recurrence for the border tower with elementary coefficients:

\[ \boxed{\;\Do_{h,\ell',0}(q) = a_{\ell'}(q)\,\Do_{h,\ell'-1,0}(q) + b_{\ell'}(q)\,\Do_{h,\ell'-1,0}(q{-}1) + c_{\ell'}(q)\,\Do_{h,\ell'-2,0}(q)\;} \tag{P3′} \] \[ a_{\ell'} = \frac{\gamma(2\ell'{+}1)}{\ell'\,p}\,\frac{\mathcal J_{\ell'}(q{-}1)}{\mathcal J_{\ell'-1}(q{-}1)}, \quad b_{\ell'} = -\frac{2\ell'{+}1}{\ell'\,p}\,\frac{\mathcal J_{\ell'}(q{-}1)}{\mathcal J_{\ell'-1}(q{-}2)}, \quad c_{\ell'} = -\frac{(\ell'{-}1)(2\ell'{+}1)}{\ell'(2\ell'{-}3)}\,\frac{\mathcal J_{\ell'}(q{-}1)}{\mathcal J_{\ell'-2}(q{-}1)} \quad(c_1=0). \]

The first middle step is a clean two-term raise,

\[ \Do_{h,1,0}(q) = \frac{3}{p^2}\big(\gamma J_{1+q}-J_{2+q}\big) \Big[\frac{\gamma}{J_{1+q}}\Do_{h00}(q) - \frac{1}{J_q}\Do_{h00}(q{-}1)\Big]. \tag{P3′₁} \]

harness 39: T3 general \(\ell'\le5\), dev 1.7×10⁻⁴¹; T2 axis closed form, dev 6.9×10⁻³⁹; T4 (P3′₁) dev 1.7×10⁻⁴¹

So P3 is a linear \(\ell'\)-recurrence whose coefficients reference only the axis brackets \(\mathcal J_a\) — no Doppler operator survives in them, and the off-diagonal family \(X\) (N10 page) is never needed. What genuinely cannot be removed is sharper than "nonlinearity": (i) the single spectral shift \(q\to q{-}1\) is essential — at fixed \(q\), \(\Do_{h,\ell',0}(q)\) is not in the span of the lower middle levels, so no fixed-\(q\) linear middle recurrence exists (harness 39, T7: fixed-\(q\) residual \(0.61\); with the \(q{-}1\) levels adjoined, residual \(0\)); and (ii) the elementary form is special to the axis and the middle border — raising an outer or inner index off the axis needs a column ratio that is a true ratio of Doppler operators, not a bracket (harness 39, T6: the axis coefficient fails off-axis by \(O(1)\)), where the rank-one fill (P2) is the right tool. The end-to-end generation is on the N25 page.

The repeated ladder steps can also be summed in closed form. That is structure theory — it explains the shape of the family — but it introduces auxiliary weight families and is not needed to generate anything, so it is kept out of the way on a separate page: Summing the ladder (structure theory).

5  Reflection and rank-one fill — N1, P2

Reflection (N1). Claim \(\;\Do_{h\ell'l}(q)=\Do_{l\ell'h}(-3-q)\). Proof. Expand both sides by (1.2):

\[ \Do_{h\ell'l}(q) = \tfrac1\gamma\,{}^{-1-q}\Kk_{h\ell'}(+\beta)\,{}^{-q}\Kk_{\ell'l}(-\beta), \qquad \Do_{l\ell'h}(-3-q) = \tfrac1\gamma\,{}^{2+q}\Kk_{l\ell'}(+\beta)\,{}^{3+q}\Kk_{\ell'h}(-\beta). \]

Apply the transposition (2.3), in the form \({}^{d}\Kk_{\ell\ell'}(+\beta)={}^{2-d}\Kk_{\ell'\ell}(-\beta)\), to each factor of the right-hand side: \({}^{2+q}\Kk_{l\ell'}(+\beta)={}^{-q}\Kk_{\ell'l}(-\beta)\) and \({}^{3+q}\Kk_{\ell'h}(-\beta)={}^{-1-q}\Kk_{h\ell'}(+\beta)\). These are exactly the two factors of the left-hand side; since the scalar kernels commute, the two sides coincide. \(\blacksquare\) harness 37, N1 → 0 (operator form \(\Do(\Oh)=\Do^{\mathsf T}(3-\Oh)\); reflection page).

Rank-one fill (P2). Claim \(\;\Do_{h\ell'l}(q)=\Do_{h\ell'0}(q)\,\Do_{0\ell'l}(q)/\Do_{0\ell'0}(q)\). Proof. Write \(F_x={}^{-1-q}\Kk_{x\ell'}(+\beta)\), \(G_y={}^{-q}\Kk_{\ell'y}(-\beta)\), so that \(\Do_{x\ell'y}=\gamma^{-1}F_x G_y\). Then

\[ \frac{\Do_{h\ell'0}\,\Do_{0\ell'l}}{\Do_{0\ell'0}} = \frac{(\gamma^{-1}F_h G_0)(\gamma^{-1}F_0 G_l)}{\gamma^{-1}F_0 G_0} = \gamma^{-1}F_h G_l = \Do_{h\ell'l}, \]

the shared border factors \(F_0, G_0\) cancelling. This is the rank-one (separable) structure of (1.2) at fixed \(\ell'\). \(\blacksquare\) harness 37, P2 → 0; harness 34, P2, exact

Together P0, P3, N1, P2 generate every \(\Do_{h\ell'l}(q)\) from the seed \(\Do_{000}\), with the physical Doppler operator the only object at every step. As a check, this scheme rederives the \(\Do_{020}\) and \(\Do_{121}\) closed forms exactly, matching the published expressions as symbolic identities (harness 35).
