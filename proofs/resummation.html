1  Summing the ladder — a structural corollary

Not required for generation. The relations of §3–§5 (P0, P3, N1, P2) together with the seed already generate every element explicitly, using only \(\gamma,p\), the brackets \(J_w\) and the constants \(C_\ell\) — see the recurrence-form page, where all 216 elements are produced from \(\Do_{000}\) alone (harness 45, dev 0). This subsection instead sums the repeated ladder steps in closed form. That is worthwhile as structure theory — it explains the shape of the family and identifies the weights with the published bracket coefficients — but it introduces auxiliary weight families, so it is a corollary rather than a prerequisite.

Because (P3′) is linear with coefficients that do not involve the outer index, it can be unrolled and summed in closed form: one need not step the ladder at all. Each application contributes at most one spectral shift, so level \(\ell'\) reaches exactly \(\ell'+1\) shifts. Writing the normalised axis element

\[ \widehat{\Do}_a(q)\;:=\;\frac{\Do_{a00}(q)}{J_{q+1}}, \]

which strips the monopole seed factor, the border tower collapses onto the axis family:

\[ \boxed{\;\Do_{h\ell'0}(q)\;=\;A_{\ell'}(q)\sum_{j=0}^{\ell'}c^{(\ell')}_j\,\widehat{\Do}_h(q-j)\;} \tag{R} \]

with an \(h\)-independent prefactor \(A_{\ell'}\) and weights \(c^{(\ell')}_j\) that are elementary in \(\gamma,p\) — they contain no \(J\) whatsoever — and, crucially, are independent of the outer index \(h\). The first two levels are

\[ c^{(1)}=\Big\{1,\;-\tfrac{1}{\gamma}\Big\},\qquad c^{(2)}\;\propto\;\big\{3+2p^2,\;-6\gamma,\;3\big\}, \]

and the second row is precisely the bracket of the published \(\Do_{020}\) closed form: the resummation weights are the axis-bracket coefficients. Feeding (R) into the rank-one fill (P2) with reflection (N1) gives the master formula — every element in closed form, straight from the axis family:

\[ \boxed{\;\Do_{\ell\ell'\ell''}(q)= \frac{\Big[\sum_{j}W^{(\ell')}_j(q)\,\Do_{\ell00}(q-j)\Big]\; \Big[\sum_{k}W^{(\ell')}_k(-3-q)\,\Do_{\ell''00}(-3-q-k)\Big]} {\sum_{m}W^{(\ell')}_m(q)\,\Do_{000}(q-m)}\;} \tag{M} \]

so the entire three-index family is generated by the one-index axis family \(\Do_{a00}\) — itself climbed from the seed \(\Do_{000}\) by (P0) — with all \(\ell'\)-dependence carried by elementary scalar weights acting as spectral shifts \(q\to q-j\). The three indices decouple: \(\ell\) and \(\ell''\) enter only through \(\Do_{\ell00}\) and \(\Do_{\ell''00}\), and \(\ell'\) only through the weights.

2  Proof of the resummation, by induction on \(\ell'\)

Theorem. With \(\widehat{\Do}_a(q)=\Do_{a00}(q)/J_{q+1}\), for every \(\ell'\ge0\) and every outer index \(h\),

\[ \Do_{h\ell'0}(q)=\mathcal J_{\ell'}(q-1)\sum_{j=0}^{\ell'}U^{(\ell')}_j\,\widehat{\Do}_h(q-j), \]

where the weights \(U^{(\ell')}_j\) are elementary in \(\gamma,p\) — free of \(J\), of \(q\) and of \(h\) — generated by \(U^{(0)}_0=p\) and

\[ U^{(\ell)}_j=\alpha_\ell\,U^{(\ell-1)}_j+\beta_\ell\,U^{(\ell-1)}_{j-1}+\kappa_\ell\,U^{(\ell-2)}_j,\qquad \alpha_\ell=\frac{\gamma(2\ell+1)}{\ell p},\quad \beta_\ell=-\frac{2\ell+1}{\ell p},\quad \kappa_\ell=-\frac{(\ell-1)(2\ell+1)}{\ell(2\ell-3)} , \]

with \(U^{(\ell)}_j=0\) outside \(0\le j\le\ell\). So the prefactor of (R) is identified: \(A_{\ell'}=\mathcal J_{\ell'}(q-1)\).

Proof. Base. \(\Do_{h00}(q)=J_{q+1}\widehat{\Do}_h(q)\) by definition, and \(\mathcal J_0(q-1)=J_{q+1}/p\); taking \(U^{(0)}_0=p\) the right-hand side is \(J_{q+1}\widehat{\Do}_h(q)\). ✓

Step. Assume the statement at levels \(\ell'-1\) and \(\ell'-2\) (identically in \(q\)). Apply (P3′), whose coefficients \(a_{\ell'},b_{\ell'},c_{\ell'}\) do not involve \(h\). The middle term uses the hypothesis at argument \(q-1\), which is legitimate as it holds identically in \(q\); reindexing \(j\to j+1\) there produces the \(\beta\)-shift and extends the support by exactly one, giving \(0\le j\le\ell'\). The three prefactors then collapse onto a single bracket, because

\[ a_{\ell'}\mathcal J_{\ell'-1}(q{-}1)=\alpha_{\ell'}\mathcal J_{\ell'}(q{-}1),\quad b_{\ell'}\mathcal J_{\ell'-1}(q{-}2)=\beta_{\ell'}\mathcal J_{\ell'}(q{-}1),\quad c_{\ell'}\mathcal J_{\ell'-2}(q{-}1)=\kappa_{\ell'}\mathcal J_{\ell'}(q{-}1), \]

which is exactly the shape of the (P3′) coefficients in §4.1 — each is \(\mathcal J_{\ell'}(q{-}1)\) divided by the bracket of the level it multiplies. Dividing through by \(\mathcal J_{\ell'}(q-1)\) therefore removes every \(J\) and leaves precisely the \(U\)-recursion, whose coefficients \(\alpha,\beta,\kappa\) are elementary and \(q\)-free. Since \(U^{(0)}\) is elementary and \(q\)-free, so is every \(U^{(\ell')}\) — whence the \(q\)-shift in the \(\beta\)-term acts trivially on the weights, and \(h\)-independence propagates because nothing in the step refers to \(h\). \(\blacksquare\)

The weights are, up to an overall scalar per level,

\[ \begin{aligned} U^{(1)}&\propto\{\gamma,\,-1\}, &\qquad U^{(2)}&\propto\{3+2p^2,\,-6\gamma,\,3\},\\ U^{(3)}&\propto\{\gamma(5+2p^2),\,-3(5+4p^2),\,15\gamma,\,-5\}, &\qquad U^{(4)}&\propto\{35+40p^2+8p^4,\,-20\gamma(7+4p^2),\,30(7+6p^2),\,-140\gamma,\,35\}, \end{aligned} \]

the \(\gamma\)'s alternating in step with the parity law (N2), and \(U^{(2)}\) being exactly the bracket of the published \(\Do_{020}\) closed form. harness 42: T1 prefactor collapses to \(\ell=10\); T2 base case; T3/T4 elementarity and support \(\ell'+1\) to \(\ell=10\); T5 the theorem as a symbolic identity, dev 0, \(h\le6,\ \ell'\le8\); T7 \(U^{(2)}\) = published \(\Do_{020}\) bracket; T9 closure against the kernel definition, dev 0

3  Collapsing the ladder onto the seed

Not needed for generation. Everything above is already complete and explicit: the four relations plus the seed generate every element with no further input. What follows is a separate structural observation — that the repeated ladder steps can be summed in closed form — which is useful for understanding why the family has the shape it does, but introduces auxiliary weight families (\(U^{(\ell')},\rho^{(a)},\mathcal J_a\)) that you would otherwise have to compute. If you simply want the operators, use the explicit relations above and stop here.

The axis ladder resums exactly like the middle one. Writing the normalised element \(\widehat{\Do}_a(q)=\Do_{a00}(q)/J_{q+1}\), unrolling (P0) gives

\[ \widehat{\Do}_a(q)=\sum_{k=0}^{a}\rho^{(a)}_k\,\widehat{\Do}_0(q+k), \]

and the two weight families turn out to be the same one up to a trivial scalar,

\[ \rho^{(n)}=\frac{U^{(n)}}{\sqrt{2n+1}\;p}, \]

so a single elementary family governs both ladders. Composing the axis and middle resummations, the double sum collapses to a cross-correlation of the two weight vectors:

\[ \boxed{\;\Do_{h\ell'0}(q)=\mathcal J_{\ell'}(q-1)\sum_{m=-\ell'}^{h}A^{(h,\ell')}_m\,\widehat{\Do}_0(q+m), \qquad A^{(h,\ell')}_m=\sum_{j}U^{(\ell')}_j\,\rho^{(h)}_{j+m}\;} \tag{S} \]

Every element is therefore a finite, explicit combination of the single seed \(\widehat{\Do}_0(x)=\Do_{000}(x)/J_{x+1}\) at integer-shifted spectral index. Closing with (P2)+(N1) — where the prefactor \(\mathcal J_{\ell'}(q-1)\) cancels between the first border and the denominator — the top element reads

\[ \boxed{\;\Do_{555}(q)=\mathcal J_{5}(-4-q)\, \frac{\Big[\sum_{m=-5}^{5}A_m\widehat{\Do}_0(q+m)\Big]\Big[\sum_{m=-5}^{5}A_m\widehat{\Do}_0(-3-q+m)\Big]} {\sum_{j=0}^{5}U^{(5)}_j\,\widehat{\Do}_0(q-j)}\;} \]

with \(A_m=\sum_j U^{(5)}_j\rho^{(5)}_{j+m}\) and

\[ U^{(5)}\propto\big\{\gamma(63{+}56p^2{+}8p^4),\;-15(21{+}28p^2{+}8p^4),\;210\gamma(3{+}2p^2),\; -70(9{+}8p^2),\;315\gamma,\;-63\big\}. \]

So \(\Do_{555}\) — like every other element — is written entirely in terms of \(\Do_{000}\) evaluated at \(q,\,q\pm1,\dots,q\pm5\) (and the reflected arguments), with elementary \(\gamma,p\) coefficients.
